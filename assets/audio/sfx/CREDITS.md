# Recorded sound effects

All clips here are cut and processed from public-domain (CC0) recordings by `tools/audio/build_sfx.py`.

- **Gunshots** (`shot_*`): *The Free Firearm Sound Library*, Still North Media, CC0 —
  https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
  (Walther PPQ, 1911, Carl Gustav M45, PPSh, AR-15, Savage 10, SKS, AK-47, Mosin-Nagant, Tikka T3, Marlin 336,
  Winchester 1894 / Model 12, Benelli Nova, Charles Daly). Each take layers a close recording with the same gun
  from a distance; suppressed shots are filtered close shots.
- **Reload foley** (`mag_out`, `mag_in`, `bolt`): *Gun reload sounds*, Joaken, CC0 —
  https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
